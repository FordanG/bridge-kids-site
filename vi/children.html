This notice

This is the children's privacy notice for Bridge Kids, including the notice for the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). It is part of the privacy policy. The data retention policy below is the written retention policy for the app.

Please read it as a draft. It has not had legal review, and it is not in force.

Operator

The operator is Gabriel John Fordan, an individual developer. He is not a store, a school, or a childcare service. The public email is gabrieljohnfordan@gmail.com. The postal address and telephone number are not finished. A COPPA policy usually includes those as well, so this notice cannot be published as final until legal review fills or formally resolves that gap. See Contact.

Directed to children

Bridge Kids is an app for children aged 3–10. A parent or guardian sets it up and uses the parent area. The child uses the child area. On the App Store, the app is in the Kids category under Apple's "Ages 5 & Under" band. It is designed for children aged 3–10.

The child is not asked to create an account, to type an email address, or to accept these terms. There is no account. A grown-up sets up the app, behind a parental gate. The gate does not ask for a birth year.

Personal information

The operator does not collect personal information from children. The app does not send profile information, learning records, or device identifiers to the operator. It does not use the microphone and does not record audio. There is no Bridge Kids account and no Bridge Kids server.

The app can store a profile and a learning record on the device itself. That information is described in the next section so a parent can see it. It is not transmitted to us. Whether any law still treats that on-device storage as "collection" is a question for counsel before release. The App Store privacy form is planned as "Data Not Collected," and that answer has to be checked against this disclosure.

On the device

Private storage on the device can hold:

  • a nickname, an avatar, an age band for a child aged 3–10, a learning direction, and an optional start date, for up to four profiles
  • progress, a record of which words your child knows and is still practising, and collected stickers
  • parent-area settings, including the language (English or Japanese) and the daily play-time limit (one daily allowance per device), and today's play time

A parent can see progress in the parent area. The app is free, so the child area does not show a price.

Identifiers

The app does not read the advertising identifier, does not read the identifier for vendors, and does not use a tracking SDK. COPPA treats persistent identifiers as personal information. The design does not collect them for the operator.

Parents' rights

A parent can refuse to let the child use the app. A parent can review what the app stores by looking at the parent area on the device: we cannot show a remote copy, because we do not have one. A parent can delete a profile on the device, or delete the app.

A parent can refuse future collection. There is no collection by the operator to turn off. There is also no account to close.

A parent may write to gabrieljohnfordan@gmail.com to ask what we hold. Do not include a child's full name, exact birth date, photograph, or voice recording. We do not need them to answer. The answer the design allows is that we do not hold the child's information.

Data retention policy

This is the written data retention policy for Bridge Kids. It covers children and parents.

On our systems

We retain nothing. The publisher does not operate a Bridge Kids server, account system, analytics store, or support database. We do not retain personal information or persistent identifiers, because we never receive them. There is no retention schedule for a database we do not have, and no practice of keeping a child's file "just in case."

On the device

  • A profile and its learning record remain until the parent deletes that profile, the app is uninstalled, or the operating system deletes the app's storage, whichever comes first.
  • The app excludes that data from iOS backup. It is not retained in iCloud, and it is not copied onto a new device.

Support messages

Messages to the support address, gabrieljohnfordan@gmail.com, are kept only as long as needed to answer them and are deleted within 90 days after your request is resolved. Do not send a child's personal information. The publisher must not copy any such message into an app database. There is nowhere in the product to put it.

What this policy does not delete

We cannot delete a record held by Apple, and we cannot reach into a device. Device data is deleted by the parent, on the device, as described next.

How to delete

  1. Open the grown-ups area and complete the parental gate.
  2. Delete the child profile. The labels in the app can still change before release. The action is profile deletion in the parent area. Deleting a profile removes that profile's nickname, avatar, age band, direction, start date, and learning record from the device.
  3. Or remove the app from the device. That removes the app's local data, including every profile on that device.

Steps for the parent area are on the support page.

We cannot perform this deletion for you, because we never receive the profile. That limit is a result of not collecting the data.

Apple

Apple distributes the app on the App Store and may process optional diagnostics under its own policies. We do not receive the child's profile from Apple. We do not allow a third-party advertising or analytics SDK to receive it either. Nothing in this notice authorizes a third party to collect personal information from a child through Bridge Kids.
