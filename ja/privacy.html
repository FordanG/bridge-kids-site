The short version

  • Bridge Kids does not collect personal information. We have no accounts and no servers for this app.
  • The app does not include analytics, advertising, attribution, or crash-reporting software.
  • Speech recognition runs on the device. If that is not available, the app only notes that the child tried. Audio is not stored and is not sent to us.
  • Parents pay Apple or Google. We never see the payment card or the billing address.
  • Profiles and progress stay on the device. The app excludes them from backups and from device-to-device transfer. They do not follow a child to a new device.
  • A parent can delete a profile on the device. Removing the app removes its local data. Cancel a subscription in the store, or it can renew.
  • The written data retention policy is in the Children's privacy and COPPA notice.

Who we are

Bridge Kids is published by Gabriel John Fordan, an individual developer. In this policy, "we" and "the publisher" mean that person. We are not Apple and we are not Google. Their stores, payment services, and operating systems follow their own policies.

This draft does not name a company. Whether any legal entity other than the individual publisher should appear here is unresolved.

What the app is

Bridge Kids is an English-learning app for young children, used with a parent or guardian, on iPhone, iPad, and Android. A parent sets the app up. The child uses a separate child area. Purchases, subscription management, settings, deleting a profile, and links to these pages are behind a parental gate. The gate does not ask for a birth date or a birth year.

A parent can keep up to four child profiles on one device. A profile can hold a nickname, an avatar chosen in the app, an age band, a learning direction, and an optional start date. The age bands in the app are about 3 to 5 and about 6 to 10. Apple's and Google's store age labels are different from those bands. The exact store age selection is not final in this draft.

There are two learning directions. One uses Japanese speech on the device. The other uses American English speech on the device. The parent area can be shown in Japanese, English, Simplified Chinese, Brazilian Portuguese, Filipino, Vietnamese, or Nepali. This website uses that same list. Only the English text has been written.

The child area does not show prices. The app is designed without streak counters and without prompts that push a child toward a purchase.

What we do not collect

The app is built so that it does not send personal information to us, and we have no Bridge Kids server that could receive it. We do not collect:

  • names, nicknames, avatars, age bands, or any other profile field
  • progress, scores, attempts, or learning records
  • microphone audio, or text produced by speech recognition
  • precise location, contacts, photos, or a camera roll
  • an email address, a telephone number, or a password for Bridge Kids (there is no account)
  • advertising identifiers or other device identifiers used for tracking, including IDFA, IDFV, and the Android advertising ID
  • analytics events, advertising measurements, or crash reports from a kit inside the app
  • payment card numbers, bank details, or billing addresses

We do not sell personal information. We do not share it with advertisers. There is no third-party analytics, crash, or advertising software in the app, and the child-facing side of the app is built without third-party code.

Information can still exist on the device, and Apple or Google can still process store and operating-system data under their own rules. Those cases are described below so they are not hidden behind the words "we do not collect."

What stays on the device

The app can save the following in private storage on the device. None of it is sent to the publisher.

Child profile

A nickname, an avatar, an age band (about 3 to 5, or about 6 to 10), the learning direction, and an optional start date. Up to four profiles.

Learning record

Progress, the on-device learning model, smileys, and speaking attempts. A parent can look at progress in the parent area. For ages about 3 to 5, a speaking try is encouraged and is not scored. For ages about 6 to 10, a lenient check runs on the device.

Settings

The parent-area language and other settings.

Subscription status

A local note of whether the store subscription is active. On iOS this note is kept in the Keychain for this device only. It is not a copy of a payment card. The store is the record of the purchase.

Microphone and speech

The microphone lets a child try saying a word or phrase. This is what the app does with that audio.

  • Recognition is on-device only. The app does not include a cloud speech service.
  • On Apple devices, the app requests on-device recognition and does not use Apple's server speech path.
  • On Android, the app uses the on-device recognizer. It does not use the Android recognizer that Google describes as likely to stream audio to remote servers.
  • The language is the language of the learning direction: Japanese, or American English.
  • Audio is handled in memory for the attempt. It is not written to disk, not added to the learning record, and not sent to us.
  • If the device produces recognition text, that text stays in memory only long enough to compare the attempt, and is then discarded. It is not written to disk and not sent to us.
  • If on-device recognition is not available, the app uses an attempt-only check that also stays on the device. It can tell that the child spoke. It does not need a transcript.
  • The operating system may download a speech model from Apple or Google the first time speech is needed. That download is between the device and Apple or Google. The child's voice is not uploaded by Bridge Kids as part of it.
  • If microphone permission is declined, the child can keep learning by listening and tapping. The permission explanation is for the parent, behind the gate. The app does not nag the child.

Purchases

An optional subscription is sold only by Apple In-App Purchase or Google Play Billing. We do not run a checkout, and we do not run a receipt server. We never receive the payment card number, the billing address, or the store's payment record.

  • The price, billing period, tax, and renewal terms are shown by the store before payment. This draft does not lock a price.
  • There is no free trial and no introductory discount in the current design.
  • Chapters 1, 2, and 4 in each direction work without a subscription. A subscription unlocks the other chapters, both directions, and up to four profiles on that device.
  • Calling the subscription a family plan means those profiles on that device. Apple Family Sharing for the subscription is off. The purchase does not spread to other Apple IDs through Family Sharing, and it does not move between iOS and Android. There is no Bridge Kids account to carry it.
  • On Android, if the store cannot be reached, the current design can keep paid chapters available on that device for seven days after the last on-device check, and then show them as sleeping. Downloaded material is not deleted.
  • A purchase can sit pending while the store waits for a parent's approval. The child area still does not show a price.
  • Restore and subscription management are in the parent area, behind the gate. Cancellation is done with Apple or Google. See Support.

Downloads and the network

Content packs are hosted by Apple and Google, not by us. After a pack is on the device, the child area is meant to work offline. The device may still contact Apple or Google to download or update a pack, to complete or restore a purchase, or to download an on-device speech model. We do not operate those connections, and we do not see the child's profile in them.

Backups and a new device

Profiles, progress, and settings are stored so the operating system will not copy them into a backup or a device-to-device transfer.

  • On iOS, the app's data directory is excluded from backup.
  • On Android, backup is turned off, and the current design also excludes the data from cloud backup and from device transfer.

If the device is replaced, restored from a backup, or the app is removed, the profiles and progress do not come back. This is intentional. A subscription can still be restored from the same store account, because the store keeps the purchase. We cannot restore progress, because we never received it.

Children

Bridge Kids is directed to children. The Children's privacy and COPPA notice is part of this policy. It includes the data retention policy. A parent should read it.

We do not knowingly receive personal information from a child, or from anyone else, through the app. We do not have a database to search if a parent asks what we hold. The profile is on the device, and the parent can delete it there.

How long information stays

The full data retention policy is in the children's notice. In short:

  • We do not retain personal information on a server. We do not collect it, and we do not run a Bridge Kids server.
  • On the device, a profile and its learning record stay until a parent deletes that profile, the app is removed, or the operating system clears the app's storage.
  • Microphone audio and recognition text are not kept after the speaking attempt.
  • Apple or Google keep store purchases under their own rules. We do not keep a payment record.
  • No support inbox is published yet. There is no support database.

Store diagnostics

The app does not include a crash-reporting or analytics library, and it does not send a crash log to us.

Apple and Google can still produce their own diagnostics. An App Store user can opt in to share analytics with developers. Google Play can show Android vitals for users who opted in to usage and diagnostics. Those programs belong to the stores. We do not combine them with a child profile, because the profile never reaches us. How the store privacy forms should describe this, next to the planned answers "Data Not Collected" and "No data collected or shared," is open for legal review before release.

Store rules and privacy laws

This section says which rules the product was designed against. It is not a legal opinion, and it is not a statement that a regulator or a store has approved the app. Counsel still has to review the finished app before each storefront.

Apple Kids Category

The child area is for children. Links out, purchases, and parent settings sit behind a parental gate. The app does not include third-party advertising or third-party analytics, and it does not send personal information or device identifiers to the publisher or to an advertising network.

Google Play Families

The app does not use the advertising ID. Microphone audio stays on the device, as described above. The Families disclosure and the Data safety form still have to be completed from the finished app.

COPPA

The Children's Online Privacy Protection Act, including the amended rule that required compliance by 22 April 2026. Persistent identifiers can be personal information. The app is designed not to collect personal information, including those identifiers. The written retention policy is in the children's notice. The operator's public email, postal address, and telephone number are not finished. See the contact section.

GDPR children's rules and the UK Age Appropriate Design Code

Article 8 of the GDPR, and the UK code, set extra care around children, including limits on nudge design. We do not receive children's personal data. The app is designed without streak pressure and without purchase prompts to the child. Whether on-device storage, speech-model downloads, or store billing create any duty before an EU or UK release, including a data-protection assessment, is open.

APPI (Japan)

The Act on the Protection of Personal Information, including under-16 changes in the 2026 amendment. The start of those changes depends on a cabinet order. The design does not collect personal information. That order still has to be watched.

Brazil, ECA Digital

The Digital Child and Adolescent Statute, Law No. 15.211 of 2025, in force on 17 March 2026, with enforcement discussed from January 2027. The design does not collect children's data. Offering the app in Brazil needs its own legal review, including any age-assurance step the stores apply.

Vietnam Personal Data Protection Law

Vietnam's Personal Data Protection Law, in force on 1 January 2026, includes parental-consent rules for younger children. The design does not collect personal data. Offering the app in Vietnam needs its own legal review.

Choices a parent has

  • Refuse the microphone. Listening and tapping still work.
  • Delete one child profile in the parent area, behind the parental gate. That removes that profile from the device. It does not cancel a subscription.
  • Remove the app. That removes the app's local data. Cancel the subscription with Apple or Google, or it can renew.
  • Ask what we hold. When an address is published, write to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. The design allows only one honest answer: we do not hold the child's information. Do not send a child's full name, date of birth, or a recording. We do not need them, and this draft has no secure place to put them.

There is no password to reset and no cloud copy of progress to download.

Changes

If this policy changes after it is published, the date at the top will change and the public page will be updated. We have no accounts, so we cannot email users about a change. The app is designed to open this page from the parent area, behind the parental gate, once the site is published.
