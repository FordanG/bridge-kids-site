This draft

These are draft terms for the Bridge Kids apps. They are not in force, they have not had legal review, and they are not legal advice. The English text is the source. A browser translation is not a set of terms.

Do not treat a missing clause as a promise. Several decisions are still open, including governing law, the public contact details, and the final subscription price. They are marked where they come up.

Who offers the app

Bridge Kids is offered by Gabriel John Fordan, an individual developer. If you accept the published terms, the agreement is between you and that publisher. It is not an agreement with Apple, and it is not an agreement with Google, except for the store terms you already have with them.

Agreeing

A parent or legal guardian agrees to these terms for a child and for the store account that pays. The child is not asked to accept a contract. If you are a child reading this, please hand the device to a grown-up before using anything behind the parental gate.

Once these terms are published and in force, do not download or use the app if you do not agree to them. Store rules may also require you to accept the store's own terms before you can install the app.

The app

Bridge Kids is an English-learning app for young children on iPhone, iPad, and Android. It is a learning aid. It is not a school, not a medical device, and not a childcare service. We do not promise a learning result, a reading level, or a test score.

The child area is separate from the parent area. Up to four child profiles can exist on one device. Two learning directions exist: one with Japanese speech on the device, and one with American English speech on the device. Chapters 1, 2, and 4 in each direction are the free chapters in the current design.

The app, its artwork, audio, animation, and text are owned by the publisher or used with permission. You may not copy the content packs, resell access, or present the app as your own product. You may not reverse engineer the app except where a statute forbids that limit.

Apple, App Store, iOS, iPadOS, and iPhone are marks of Apple. Google, Android, and Google Play are marks of Google. They are named here to identify the stores. The publisher is not affiliated with Apple or Google.

Parents and children

You are responsible for the device, the store account, the subscription, and for deciding whether a child may use the app. The parental gate stands in front of purchases, subscription management, settings, profile deletion, and links to these pages. The gate does not ask for a birth year. It reduces the chance that a child acts alone. It is not a promise that a determined person cannot get past it.

The child area does not show prices. Do not use the parent area to show a child a checkout.

No accounts

Bridge Kids does not have user accounts. There is nothing to register. The Apple ID or Google account that pays is the store's account, under the store's terms. A subscription does not create a Bridge Kids profile in the cloud.

Subscriptions and billing

Optional paid access is an auto-renewable subscription billed only by Apple or Google. We do not take payment-card data, and we do not run a receipt server.

  • The store displays the price, the currency, tax, the billing period, and the renewal terms before you confirm. Those store terms control the charge. This draft does not state a price, because the price is not final.
  • There is no free trial and no introductory price in the current design.
  • Chapters 1, 2, and 4 in each direction do not need a subscription. A subscription unlocks the other chapters, both directions, and up to four profiles on that device.
  • "Family plan" means those profiles on the device tied to the store account that paid. It does not mean Apple Family Sharing. Family Sharing for this subscription is off. The subscription does not move to another Apple ID through Family Sharing, and it does not move between iOS and Android.
  • The subscription renews until you cancel it in the store account. Deleting the app or a profile does not cancel it. How to cancel is on the support page.
  • On Android, the current design allows about seven days of offline use of paid chapters after the last on-device check, and then shows those chapters as sleeping. Downloaded material is not deleted.
  • Refunds follow the store's rules. Ask the store that charged you. We cannot see the payment record.
  • Who the merchant of record is can differ by store and by country. This draft does not decide that question. The store's checkout is the authority for the charge you see.

Privacy

The privacy policy and the children's privacy and COPPA notice describe how little information leaves the device. They include the data retention policy. Using the app after those documents are in force means the parent has had a chance to read them. The child's information is not the price of a subscription: the app is designed not to send it to us.

Acceptable use

Use the app for the child's learning, under a parent's supervision. Do not use it to advertise to children, to collect information from children, or to bypass the parental gate in order to put a checkout in front of a child. Do not interfere with the content-pack checks that confirm a pack came from the publisher's release process. Do not send us a child's personal information.

Availability

We may change, pause, or withdraw the app or a chapter. Content packs depend on Apple and Google hosting them. Offline use depends on a download that already finished. We do not promise that a store, a network, or an on-device speech model will be available. Progress stored only on the device can be lost if the device is lost, replaced, or restored from a backup. That loss is a consequence of excluding the data from backups. The privacy policy states it in plain language.
