This draft

These are draft terms for the Bridge Kids apps. They are not in force, they have not had legal review, and they are not legal advice. The English text is the source. A browser translation is not a set of terms.

Do not treat a missing clause as a promise. Several decisions are still open, including governing law and whether a postal address must be published. They are marked where they come up. The public email is in the contact section.

Who offers the app

Bridge Kids is offered by Gabriel John Fordan, an individual developer. If you accept the published terms, the agreement is between you and that publisher. It is not an agreement with Apple, except for the App Store terms you already have with Apple.

Agreeing

A parent or legal guardian agrees to these terms for a child. The child is not asked to accept a contract. If you are a child reading this, please hand the device to a grown-up before using anything behind the parental gate.

Once these terms are published and in force, do not download or use the app if you do not agree to them. Store rules may also require you to accept the store's own terms before you can install the app.

The app

Bridge Kids is an app for children aged 3–10 on iPhone and iPad. The app id is com.vendrato.bridgekids. The app is free. It is a learning aid. It is not a school, not a medical device, and not a childcare service. We do not promise a learning result, a reading level, or a test score.

The child area is separate from the parent area. Up to four child profiles can exist on one device. Two learning directions exist: one with Japanese speech on the device, and one with American English speech on the device.

Parents and children

You are responsible for the device and for deciding whether a child may use the app. The parental gate stands in front of settings, profile deletion, and links to these pages. The gate does not ask for a birth year. It reduces the chance that a child acts alone. It is not a promise that a determined person cannot get past it.

The app is free. The child area does not show a price.

No accounts

Bridge Kids does not have user accounts. There is nothing to register. Installing the app from the App Store does not create a Bridge Kids profile in the cloud.

Privacy

The privacy policy and the children's privacy and COPPA notice describe how little information leaves the device. They include the data retention policy. Using the app after those documents are in force means the parent has had a chance to read them. The app is designed not to send a child's information to us.

Acceptable use

Use the app for the child's learning, under a parent's supervision. Do not use it to advertise to children, to collect information from children, or to bypass the parental gate. Do not interfere with the content-pack checks that confirm a pack came from the publisher's release process. Do not send us a child's personal information.

Availability

We may change, pause, or withdraw the app or a chapter. Content packs depend on Apple hosting them. Offline use depends on a download that already finished. We do not promise that the App Store, a network, or an on-device speech model will be available. Progress stored only on the device can be lost if the device is lost, replaced, or restored from a backup. That loss is a consequence of excluding the data from backups. The privacy policy states it in plain language.

To the extent a mandatory rule does not say otherwise, the app is provided as available, without a warranty of uninterrupted service or of a particular learning outcome, and the publisher is not liable for progress that existed only on the device or for a store outage. Nothing in this paragraph limits liability that the law says cannot be limited, including liability for fraud or for death or personal injury caused by negligence where that limit is forbidden.

If a term in this draft is unenforceable, the rest of the draft remains for review. That sentence does not make the draft binding.
