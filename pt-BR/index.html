Privacy policy, children's privacy notice, support, and terms for Bridge Kids, an app for children aged 3–10 on iPhone and iPad.

Bridge Kids is published by Gabriel John Fordan, an individual developer. The app is free. It is designed not to collect personal information and not to use accounts or telemetry. It does not use the microphone, and it does not connect to the internet. Profiles and progress stay on the device and are excluded from iOS backup.
