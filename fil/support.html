No account

Bridge Kids is an app for children aged 3–10 on iPhone and iPad. It has no sign-up, no password, and no login. This page has no form and no chat. Nothing you type in the browser is sent, because the page does not run code and has nowhere to type. We cannot look up a child or a progress record. We do not have them.

The privacy policy and the children's notice explain what stays on the device: Privacy policy, Children's privacy and COPPA notice.

The parental gate

Settings, deleting a profile, and links out to these pages are behind a gate meant for a grown-up. The gate does not ask for a birth year. It is a barrier, not a guarantee that nobody can pass it. The app is free, so the child area has no price to show.

Delete a profile

  1. Open the grown-ups control from the child home or the progress map, and complete the gate.
  2. In the parent area, delete the profile you want to remove.

The exact button labels can still change before release. Deletion removes that profile's nickname, avatar, age band, learning direction, optional start date, and learning record from the device. Removing the whole app removes every profile's local data on that device.

Microphone

The microphone is optional. You can refuse it in the system permission prompt. The child can still listen and tap. The reason for the permission is shown to the parent, behind the gate. The app does not keep asking the child.

To change the choice later, use the system settings for Bridge Kids. The app id is com.vendrato.bridgekids. Turn the microphone on or off there. That switch belongs to the operating system.

Speech

Speaking is recognized on the device, in Japanese or in American English, matching the learning direction. Audio is not saved. If the device cannot run on-device recognition, the app still accepts the attempt and does not send audio to a server. A child aged 3–5 is not scored. A child aged 6–10 gets a lenient on-device check.

Offline use

After the content packs have downloaded from Apple, the child area is meant to work without a connection. The child area does not show a network error. A parent handles retries from the parent area. The first setup tells the parent the download size before the packs are fetched.

A new device

Profiles and progress do not move to a new device, and they do not come back from an iOS backup. The app excludes them from backup. This is deliberate. You would set up profiles again on a new device.
