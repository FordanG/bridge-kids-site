No account

Bridge Kids is an app for children aged 3–10 on iPhone and iPad. It has no sign-up, no password, and no login. This page has no form and no chat. Nothing you type in the browser is sent, because the page does not run code and has nowhere to type. We cannot look up a child or a progress record. We do not have them.

The privacy policy and the children's notice explain what stays on the device: Privacy policy, Children's privacy and COPPA notice.

The parental gate

Settings, deleting a profile, and links out to these pages are behind a gate meant for a grown-up. The gate does not ask for a birth year. It is a barrier, not a guarantee that nobody can pass it. The app is free, so the child area has no price to show.

Delete a profile

  1. Open the grown-ups control from the child home or the progress map, and complete the gate.
  2. In the parent area, delete the profile you want to remove.

The exact button labels can still change before release. Deletion removes that profile's nickname, avatar, age band, learning direction, optional start date, and learning record from the device. Removing the whole app removes every profile's local data on that device.

Offline use

Everything the app needs is built in, so it works without an internet connection from the first launch. Nothing is downloaded after installation.

A new device

Progress and settings are saved only on this device and aren't backed up. If you delete the app or switch to a new device, your child's progress, stickers and settings will start over.
