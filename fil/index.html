Draft dated 23 October 2026. The English text is the source draft.

Privacy policy, children's privacy notice, support, and terms for Bridge Kids, an app for children aged 3–10 on iPhone and iPad.

Bridge Kids is published by Gabriel John Fordan, an individual developer. The app is free. It is designed not to collect personal information, not to use accounts or telemetry, and not to send a child's voice off the device. Speech recognition stays on the device. Profiles are excluded from iOS backup.
