The short version

  • Bridge Kids does not collect personal information. We have no accounts and no servers for this app.
  • The app does not include analytics, advertising, attribution, or crash-reporting software.
  • The app does not use the microphone and does not record audio.
  • The app is free.
  • Profiles and progress stay on the device. The app excludes them from iOS backup. They do not follow a child to a new device.
  • A parent can delete a profile on the device. Removing the app removes its local data.
  • The written data retention policy is in the Children's privacy and COPPA notice.

Who we are

Bridge Kids is published by Gabriel John Fordan, an individual developer. In this policy, "we" and "the publisher" mean that person. We are not Apple. The App Store and iOS follow Apple's own policies.

What the app is

Bridge Kids is an app for children aged 3–10, used with a parent or guardian, on iPhone and iPad. The app id is com.vendrato.bridgekids. The app is free. A parent sets the app up. The child uses a separate child area. Settings, deleting a profile, and links to these pages are behind a parental gate. The gate does not ask for a birth date or a birth year.

A parent can keep up to four child profiles on one device. A profile can hold a nickname, an avatar chosen in the app, an age band, a learning direction, and an optional start date. On the App Store, the app is listed in the Kids category. It is designed for children aged 3–10.

There are two learning directions: one teaches Japanese and one teaches American English. All lessons, pictures and audio are built into the app. The parent area is in English or Japanese.

The child area does not show a price. The app is designed without streak counters.

What we do not collect

The app is built so that it does not send personal information to us, and we have no Bridge Kids server that could receive it. We do not collect:

  • names, nicknames, avatars, age bands, or any other profile field
  • progress, scores, attempts, or learning records
  • audio or voice recordings (the app does not use the microphone)
  • precise location, contacts, photos, or a camera roll
  • an email address, a telephone number, or a password for Bridge Kids (there is no account)
  • advertising identifiers or other device identifiers used for tracking, including IDFA and IDFV
  • analytics events, advertising measurements, or crash reports from a kit inside the app
  • payment card numbers, bank details, or billing addresses

We do not sell or share personal information, including for targeted advertising. There is no third-party analytics, crash, or advertising software in the app, and the app includes no third-party SDKs.

Information can still exist on the device, and Apple can still process App Store and iOS data under its own rules. Those cases are described below so they are not hidden behind the words "we do not collect."

What stays on the device

The app can save the following in private storage on the device. None of it is sent to the publisher.

Child profile

A nickname, an avatar, an age band for a child aged 3–10, the learning direction, and an optional start date. Up to four profiles.

Learning record

Progress, a record of which words your child knows and is still practising, and collected stickers. A parent can look at progress in the parent area.

Settings

The parent-area language (English or Japanese), the daily play-time limit (one shared daily allowance per device, for all child profiles) and today's play time, and other settings.

The network

All lessons, pictures and audio are built into the app. The app does not connect to the internet and downloads nothing after it is installed. App updates come through the App Store under Apple's rules. Links to these pages open in Safari only after a grown-up passes the parental gate. Your child's data (profiles, learning records and settings) does not leave this device.

Backups and a new device

Progress and settings are saved only on this device and aren't backed up. If you delete the app or switch to a new device, your child's progress, stickers and settings will start over.

Children

Bridge Kids is directed to children. The Children's privacy and COPPA notice is part of this policy. It includes the data retention policy. A parent should read it.

We do not knowingly receive personal information from a child, or from anyone else, through the app. We do not have a database to search if a parent asks what we hold. The profile is on the device, and the parent can delete it there.

How long information stays

The full data retention policy is in the children's notice. In short:

  • We do not retain personal information on a server. We do not collect it, and we do not run a Bridge Kids server.
  • On the device, a profile and its learning record stay until a parent deletes that profile, the app is removed, or the operating system clears the app's storage.
  • Messages to the support address, gabrieljohnfordan@gmail.com, are kept only as long as needed to answer them and are deleted within 90 days after your request is resolved. They are not copied into an app database. There is no support database.

Store diagnostics

The app does not include a crash-reporting or analytics library, and it does not send a crash log to us.

Apple can still produce its own diagnostics. An App Store user can opt in to share analytics with developers. That program belongs to Apple. We do not combine it with a child profile, because the profile never reaches us. Data that Apple collects through its own programs is not part of the app's privacy label, which is "Data Not Collected."

This website

This website, https://bridgekids.vendrato.com, is a set of static pages. The host that serves it is TBD before store release.

These pages are static files. They do not set cookies, they do not run scripts, they do not load fonts or pictures from anyone else, and they do not use analytics. The site host may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and user agent, under its own policy. We do not receive those logs, and we do not use them for tracking. A browser translation of this page is not an official translation. The pages ask browsers not to translate them.

Store rules and privacy laws

This section says which rules the product was designed against. It is not a legal opinion, and it is not a statement that a regulator or a store has approved the app. Each new storefront gets its own legal review before the app is offered there.

Apple Kids Category

The child area is for children aged 3–10. Links out and parent settings sit behind a parental gate. The app is free. The app does not include third-party advertising or third-party analytics, and it does not send personal information or device identifiers to the publisher or to an advertising network.

COPPA

The Children's Online Privacy Protection Act, including the amended rule that required compliance by 22 April 2026. COPPA treats persistent identifiers as personal information. The app is designed not to collect personal information, including those identifiers. The written retention policy is in the children's notice. The public email is in the contact section. The postal address and telephone number are not finished.

GDPR children's rules and the UK Age Appropriate Design Code

Article 8 of the GDPR, and the UK code, set extra care around children, including limits on nudge design. We do not receive children's personal data. The app is free and is designed without streak pressure. Whether on-device storage creates any duty before an EU or UK release, including a data-protection assessment, is open for legal review.

APPI (Japan)

The Act on the Protection of Personal Information, including under-16 changes in the 2026 amendment. The start of those changes depends on a cabinet order. The design does not collect personal information. That order still has to be watched.

Brazil, ECA Digital

The Digital Child and Adolescent Statute, Law No. 15.211 of 2025, in force on 17 March 2026, with enforcement discussed from January 2027. The design does not collect children's data. Offering the app in Brazil needs its own legal review, including any age-assurance step the stores apply.

Vietnam Personal Data Protection Law

Vietnam's Personal Data Protection Law, in force on 1 January 2026, includes parental-consent rules for younger children. The design does not collect personal data. Offering the app in Vietnam needs its own legal review.

Choices a parent has

  • Delete one child profile in the parent area, behind the parental gate. That removes that profile from the device.
  • Remove the app. That removes the app's local data.
  • Ask what we hold. Write to gabrieljohnfordan@gmail.com. The design allows only one honest answer: we do not hold the child's information. Do not send a child's full name, date of birth, or a recording. We do not need them, and email is not a safe place for them.
  • If you email us, you can ask us to delete your message. You can also contact your local data-protection authority.

There is no password to reset and no cloud copy of progress.

Changes

If this policy changes after it is published, the date at the top will change and the public page will be updated. We have no accounts, so we cannot email users when the policy changes. The app is designed to open this page from the parent area, behind the parental gate, once the site is published.
