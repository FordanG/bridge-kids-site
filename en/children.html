This notice

This is the children's privacy notice for Bridge Kids, including the notice for the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). It is part of the privacy policy. The data retention policy below is the written retention policy for the app.

Please read it as a draft. It has not had legal review, and it is not in force.

Operator

The operator is Gabriel John Fordan, an individual developer. He is not a store, a school, or a childcare service. The public email, postal address, and telephone number are not finished. They are required content for a COPPA policy in the usual case, so this notice cannot be published as final until legal review fills or formally resolves that gap. See Contact.

Directed to children

Bridge Kids is directed to children. A parent or guardian sets it up and uses the parent area. The child uses the child area. The in-app age bands are about 3 to 5 and about 6 to 10. Store age categories use the stores' own labels and are not final in this draft.

The child is not asked to create an account, to type an email address, or to accept these terms. A grown-up does that, behind a parental gate. The gate does not ask for a birth year.

Personal information

The operator does not collect personal information from children. The app does not send profile information, learning records, audio, or device identifiers to the operator. There is no Bridge Kids account and no Bridge Kids server.

The app can store a profile and a learning record on the device itself. That information is described in the next section so a parent can see it. It is not transmitted to us. Whether any law still treats that on-device storage as "collection" is a question for counsel before release. The store privacy forms are planned as "Data Not Collected" on the App Store and "No data collected" and "No data shared" on Google Play, and those answers have to be checked against this disclosure.

On the device

Private storage on the device can hold:

  • a nickname, an avatar, an age band, a learning direction, and an optional start date, for up to four profiles
  • progress, the on-device learning model, smileys, and speaking attempts
  • parent-area settings, including language
  • a local note of subscription status, which is not payment-card data

A parent can see progress in the parent area. The child area does not show prices.

Microphone

If a parent allows the microphone, the child can try speaking. Audio stays in memory on the device for that attempt. It is not stored, not sent to the operator, and not sent to an advertising network. Recognition text, if the device creates any, is discarded when the attempt is judged. If the device cannot do on-device recognition, the app only marks that the child tried, still on the device.

The app does not use a cloud speech recognizer. A speech model may be downloaded by the operating system from Apple or Google. That is a store or system download, not a Bridge Kids upload of the child's voice.

Identifiers

The app does not read the advertising identifier on iOS or Android, does not read the identifier for vendors, and does not use a tracking SDK. The Android advertising-ID permission is not part of the app. COPPA treats persistent identifiers as personal information. The design does not collect them for the operator.

Parents' rights

A parent can refuse to let the child use the app, refuse the microphone, and still use listen-and-tap. A parent can review what the app stores by looking at the parent area on the device: we cannot show a remote copy, because we do not have one. A parent can delete a profile on the device, or delete the app.

A parent can refuse future collection. There is no collection by the operator to turn off. There is also no account to close.

When a contact address exists, a parent may write to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} to ask what we hold. Do not include a child's full name, exact birth date, photograph, or voice recording. We do not need them to answer. The answer the design allows is that we hold nothing about the child.

Data retention policy

This is the written data retention policy for Bridge Kids. It covers children and parents.

On our systems

We retain nothing. The publisher does not operate a Bridge Kids server, account system, analytics store, or support database. We do not retain personal information, persistent identifiers, audio, or transcripts, because we never receive them. There is no retention schedule for a database we do not have, and no practice of keeping a child's file "just in case."

On the device

  • A profile and its learning record remain until the parent deletes that profile, the app is uninstalled, or the operating system deletes the app's storage, whichever comes first.
  • The app excludes that data from operating-system backups and from Android device-to-device transfer. It is not retained by the app in iCloud or in Google backup, and it is not copied onto a new device.
  • Microphone audio is not retained. It exists in memory during the attempt and is then discarded. It is not written to disk.
  • Recognition text is not retained. It exists in memory only for the match and is then discarded. It is not written to disk.
  • The subscription-status note stays in private device storage until the app is removed or the store updates it. It is not a payment card. Apple or Google retain the purchase under their own rules.

Support messages

No support address is published yet. If {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} is later approved, parents should not send a child's personal information. The publisher must not copy any such message into an app database. There is nowhere in the product to put it. How long an ordinary email may remain in a mailbox is not decided. Legal review has to set that period before the address is published. Until then, do not send children's information.

What this policy does not delete

We cannot delete a record held by Apple or Google, and we cannot reach into a device. Store account and purchase records follow the store's privacy policy. Device data is deleted by the parent, on the device, as described next.

How to delete

  1. Open the grown-ups area and complete the parental gate.
  2. Delete the child profile. The labels in the app can still change before release. The action is profile deletion in the parent area. Deleting a profile removes that profile's nickname, avatar, age band, direction, start date, and learning record from the device.
  3. Or remove the app from the device. That removes the app's local data, including every profile on that device.

Deletion on the device does not cancel a subscription. Cancel with Apple or Google. Steps are on the support page.

We cannot perform this deletion for you, because we never receive the profile. That limit is a result of not collecting the data.

Apple and Google

Apple and Google process downloads, purchases, and optional diagnostics under their own policies. We do not receive the child's profile from them. We do not allow a third-party advertising or analytics SDK to receive it either. Nothing in this notice authorizes a third party to collect personal information from a child through Bridge Kids.
